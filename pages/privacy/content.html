Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

The short version

ToolHub does not collect, store, or transmit anything you enter into a tool. There are no user accounts, no server-side processing, and no analytics or advertising scripts on this site.

What happens to what you type or upload

Every tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Text you type, files you select, and settings you choose are processed locally on your device and are never sent to a ToolHub server or any third party. This site has no backend capable of receiving that data in the first place.

Local storage

Some tools remember a preference (such as your light/dark theme choice) using your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and can be cleared at any time through your browser's site data settings.

Cookies and tracking

ToolHub does not use cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising trackers of any kind.

Third-party hosting

Like any website, ToolHub's static files are served by a hosting provider, which may log standard, aggregate web-server access information (such as IP address and request timestamps) for operational and security purposes, independent of anything a tool itself does.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it.
