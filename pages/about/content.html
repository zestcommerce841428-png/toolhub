About ToolHub

ToolHub is a growing library of small, focused, browser-based tools — text utilities, developer formatters, generators, converters, and calculators. The goal is simple: whatever quick task you'd otherwise search for and click through three ad-laden pages to accomplish, ToolHub should do in one page load, with no sign-up and no waiting.

Everything runs on your device

Every tool on this site is 100% client-side JavaScript. When you format JSON, generate a password, or count words, that computation happens in your browser — nothing you type or upload is ever sent to a server. There is no backend, no database, and no account system, because none of that is necessary for what these tools do.

No tracking, no analytics

ToolHub does not run analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or any other third-party tracking. We have no way of knowing what you typed into a tool, and we've built it that way on purpose.

Built to last

This site is intentionally built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no framework runtime, no heavy dependencies. That keeps every tool fast to load and simple enough to still make sense years from now.
