Three different questions

These look similar but answer different questions: the first finds a portion of a number, the second finds what portion one number is of another, and the third finds how much a number grew or shrank in percentage terms. Mixing them up is a common source of "that math doesn't add up" mistakes — e.g. a 20% increase followed by a 20% decrease does not return you to the original number.
