Why stacked discounts aren't additive

"20% off, then an extra 10% off" is not 30% off. The second discount applies to the already-reduced price: $100 → $80 after 20% off → $72 after another 10% off — an effective 28% off, not 30%. Retailers rely on this being non-obvious; the calculator above shows the real effective percentage.
