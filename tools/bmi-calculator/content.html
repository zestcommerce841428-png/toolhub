Understanding your result

BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. Using the WHO adult thresholds: under 18.5 is Underweight, 18.5–24.9 is Normal weight, 25–29.9 is Overweight, and 30+ is Obese. It doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, age, or sex, so talk to a healthcare provider for guidance specific to you.
