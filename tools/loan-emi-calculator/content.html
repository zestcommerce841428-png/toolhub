How EMI is calculated

This uses the standard reducing-balance formula that mortgages, auto loans, and most installment loans are built on: EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the number of monthly payments. Because interest is charged on the remaining balance, each payment covers slightly less interest and slightly more principal than the one before it — visible in the amortization schedule above.
