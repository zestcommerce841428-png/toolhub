Just a URL per line, or add optional details: url, priority, changefreq, lastmod (e.g. https://example.com/, 1.0, daily, 2026-07-29).

What each field means

priority (0.0–1.0) is a hint about a page's importance relative to others on your own site — search engines treat it as a weak signal at best. changefreq is how often a page tends to change; also a hint, not a guarantee crawlers follow exactly. Neither is required — a bare list of URLs is a perfectly valid sitemap.
