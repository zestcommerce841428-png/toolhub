How this converter works

Unlike length or weight, temperature scales differ by an offset as well as a ratio — 0°C isn't 0 of anything in Fahrenheit or Kelvin — so every conversion here goes through an explicit formula rather than a single multiplier. Kelvin and Rankine are absolute scales (0 is absolute zero), which is why an impossible value like -300°C is rejected instead of silently producing a negative Kelvin reading.
