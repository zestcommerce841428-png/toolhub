How the check digit works

The 13th digit isn't part of the product code — it's calculated from the other 12 so a scanner can detect a misread. Each of the first 12 digits is multiplied by 1 or 3 (alternating), summed, and the check digit is whatever makes that sum a multiple of 10. This tool computes and encodes a genuine, scannable EAN-13 barcode using the real ISO/IEC 15420 bar-pattern tables — not a decorative stand-in image.
