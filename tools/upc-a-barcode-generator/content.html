UPC-A and EAN-13 are the same barcode, structurally

UPC-A (12 digits, standard in the US/Canada) is encoded exactly like EAN-13 (13 digits, used almost everywhere else) with an invisible leading 0 — a UPC-A scanner and an EAN-13 scanner read the same 95 bars underneath. This tool computes the real check digit and encodes a genuine, scannable barcode, not a decorative image.
