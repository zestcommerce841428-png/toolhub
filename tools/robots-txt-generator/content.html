A couple of things robots.txt can't do

Disallowing a path only asks well-behaved crawlers not to fetch it — it doesn't prevent access, and it doesn't remove a page that's already indexed (a search engine can still list a URL it was linked to without ever crawling it). For that, use a noindex meta tag on the page itself, or actual access control if it needs to stay private.
