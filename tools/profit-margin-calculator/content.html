Margin vs. markup — not the same number

Margin is profit as a percentage of your selling price; markup is profit as a percentage of your cost. Selling something for $60 that cost $40 is a 33.3% margin but a 50% markup — the same sale, two genuinely different percentages depending which base you divide by. See the Markup Calculator if you're pricing from a target markup instead.
