    What "offline" vs. "online" crack time means

    Offline assumes an attacker already has a copy of a fast, unsalted-equivalent hash and can try billions of guesses per second on dedicated hardware — the realistic worst case after a breach. Online assumes guessing through a normal login form with rate limiting, closer to a few guesses per second. The gap between the two is enormous, which is exactly why a leaked password database is so much more dangerous than someone guessing at your login page.
