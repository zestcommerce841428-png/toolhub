Why Base32 instead of Base64?

Base32 uses a 32-character alphabet (A-Z and 2-7) chosen to be unambiguous when hand-typed or read aloud — no lowercase/uppercase confusion, and it skips 0/1/8/9 to avoid look-alikes with O/I/B/g. That's why authenticator apps encode TOTP/2FA secret keys in Base32 rather than Base64: you're far more likely to need to manually type one correctly.
