Seconds vs. milliseconds

Unix timestamps traditionally count seconds since January 1, 1970 UTC (used by most server-side languages and Unix tools), while JavaScript's own Date.now() and new Date() use milliseconds — mixing the two up by a factor of 1000 is a very common bug. Double-check the unit selector matches what produced your timestamp.
