Why compounding frequency matters

A 5% annual rate compounded monthly earns slightly more than 5% compounded annually, because interest starts earning its own interest sooner — that "effective" rate is the APY shown above. The formula is A = P(1 + r/n)ⁿᵗ, where P is principal, r is the annual rate as a decimal, n is compounds per year, and t is time in years.
