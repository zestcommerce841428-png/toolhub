What AA and AAA mean

These are WCAG 2.x conformance levels. AA (4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text — 18pt+, or 14pt+ bold) is the baseline most accessibility guidelines and legal requirements target. AAA (7:1 / 4.5:1) is a stricter, optional enhanced level. The ratio itself is computed from each color's WCAG relative luminance — the same formula browsers' own accessibility inspectors use.
