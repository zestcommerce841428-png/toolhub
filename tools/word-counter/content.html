How this word counter works

Statistics update as you type, computed entirely in your browser. Reading time assumes 200 words per minute (a typical adult silent reading speed); speaking time assumes 130 words per minute (a comfortable spoken pace). Paragraphs are counted as blocks of text separated by a blank line.
