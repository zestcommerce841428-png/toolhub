KB vs. KiB — why both exist here

Storage manufacturers advertise capacity in decimal units (1 GB = 1000³ bytes), while operating systems traditionally report free space in binary units (which many still label "GB" but actually mean 1024³ bytes, more precisely called a GiB). That mismatch is exactly why a "1 TB" drive shows up as roughly 931 GB in your file manager. This converter keeps the two conventions as separate, explicitly labeled units so the ambiguity can't sneak into your result.
